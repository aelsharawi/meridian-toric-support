Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026
Meridian Toric, version 0.3.0
Publisher: ahmed alshaarawy

Meridian Toric is a free camera-first reference-photo app with local, assistive iris-circle fitting. Its core tools work offline, without an account.

Preclinical validation build. Use non-patient test images and non-identifying case codes. This version is not validated for surgical marking, ocular registration or clinical accuracy.

Information handled by the app

The app works with photos you capture or select, the selected eye, manual annotations, entered axis values, an optional case code and, when available, a device-roll reading. Saved cases contain the working photo and related case metadata on your device.

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or AI service. It does not upload photos, case codes, annotations, measurements or usage data to an app-operated server or third party.

Camera, photo selection and motion

  • Camera: camera permission allows the direct back-camera view and sequential local image samples for assistive circle fitting. Unselected samples and temporary analysis thumbnails are discarded. Nothing is uploaded. Audio is not recorded.
  • Photo import: you choose an image through the operating system’s selection interface. The app does not automatically save working photos to your photo library or delete original library images.
  • Motion: motion access supports a manual 6 o’clock guide using fused gravity and a gyroscope movement check. Processing stays on the device. Saved guided captures include direction, phone roll and capture-request time; raw sensor streams are not saved. Android requests the physical-activity permission required by the installed motion module. These readings do not measure ocular rotation.

You can manage available permissions in your device settings. Denying access can prevent the associated feature from working.

Local encryption and temporary files

Saved photos and case metadata are encrypted on your device. The encryption key is stored in the operating system’s secure key storage and is bound to the device.

Unsaved working photos and decrypted case previews temporarily exist as app-private cache files. The app removes its managed case previews when the preview closes or the app goes into the background, and clears its managed working caches on launch.

Original images in your photo library remain under your control. Deleting an app case does not delete an original photo-library image.

Saving, deletion and backups

You confirm the vessel or ink reference and choose when to save or delete a case. The fitted circle and reference type/point, available level reference and optional planned axis are saved locally. The accepted legal-notice version is stored locally so later launches open the camera directly. This version has no sharing, export, server synchronization, cloud recovery or automatic photo-library save.

Android app backup is disabled. iOS device backups may contain encrypted case files, but the device-bound encryption key is not portable to a different device. Deleting the app or its key, or losing the device, can permanently remove access to saved cases.

Saved references do not replace the clinical record. Do not enter a patient name or identifier in the optional case-code field.

External links

Core tools do not need an internet connection. External reference links open in the device’s browser rather than inside the app. Visiting an external site is subject to that site’s privacy policy.

This support and privacy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection.

These pages do not set cookies, include analytics, or provide forms. They contain no JavaScript, embedded services, external fonts or external image assets. The hosting practices described above are separate from the offline app’s handling of case information.

Support and privacy contact

Contact ahmed alshaarawy at ask@corneo.link with a technical-support or privacy question. Sending an email shares the information you include with the publisher through email services.

Do not email patient photos, names, case records or identifying details. For technical help, send only your device model, operating-system version, app version and a description without sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

This notice will be updated if a released version changes how the app handles information. The effective date above identifies this policy version.
